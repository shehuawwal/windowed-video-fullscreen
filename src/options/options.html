Windowed Video Fullscreen

Videos go "fullscreen" inside the browser tab instead of taking over the screen.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click Add shortcut, then press the keys. Each action can have more than one shortcut. Shortcuts work on the web page, so a few combinations the browser keeps for itself (such as new tab or close tab) can't be used.

Toggle windowed fullscreen

Picks the playing (or largest) visible video on the page.

    Exit windowed fullscreen

    Only acts while a video is windowed.

      Video quality

      If the player offers this quality, it switches to it. Otherwise the site's own choice is kept. Works on YouTube and on sites that use JW Player, Video.js or hls.js. Sites such as Netflix, Twitch and Vimeo keep their own quality.
